      One-click import from any recipe site

      Most sites block apps from reading their pages directly, so use this bookmarklet instead. It runs on the recipe page in your own browser, picks up the recipe, and opens it here. Nothing goes through a server.

      1. Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + B).
      2. Drag this button onto it: 📥 Save to RecipeBox
      3. On a recipe page, click the bookmark. The recipe opens here for review.
